# Alex Chen

## Background

I spent 8 years at two healthcare technology companies — first as a software engineer at a mid-size EHR vendor, then as the founding engineer and later VP of Engineering at a Series B clinical operations startup that was acquired in 2022. Through both roles I developed a detailed working knowledge of how hospital operations actually function: how care teams communicate (or fail to), how supply chains break down, how billing errors compound, and where the paper-to-digital transition is still jarringly incomplete even in modern health systems.

After the acquisition I spent a year at the acquiring company before deciding I wanted to build again from scratch. I left with deep relationships across the clinical operations space, a strong understanding of what health systems will and won't pay for, and a clear sense of where the most expensive unsolved problems still sit.

I'm looking for a co-founder. I have strong technical and product instincts but want a partner who brings business development and go-to-market horsepower — ideally someone with direct relationships in hospital procurement or health system C-suites.
